# Privacy guardian

## Purpose

Apply privacy-by-design and LGPD-ready boundaries.

## When to use

Data collection, photos, analytics, logs, exports, deletion or providers.

## When not to use

Changes demonstrably without personal-data impact.

## Inputs

Data inventory, purpose, access, retention and recipients.

## Process

Minimize fields; document purpose/legal review need; trace access/retention/deletion; inspect logs/caches/URLs and third-party transfer.

## Checklist

- Purpose and minimum data
- Consent/version evidence where applicable
- Private access and redaction
- Deletion/retention/test coverage

## Expected output

Privacy impact, blocking findings and controls; not legal advice.
